#### **2. Implementing the QI-GAN Framework (The Core Development)**

This is where you will write the code. The architecture you designed is perfect. Here's how to implement it piece by piece.

*   **Step 4: Build the Classical GAN (Baseline).**
    *   **Model Choice:** Use a **SMILES-based GAN** like **ORGAN** (Objective-Reinforced Generative Adversarial Network). It's a well-established framework in PyTorch/TensorFlow that's easy to modify.
    *   **Action:** Train this base GAN on your `training_smiles.txt` file. The goal is to get it to generate valid, drug-like SMILES strings. This will be your **Baseline Model**.

*   **Step 5: Build the Quantum Property Prediction Module (Simulated).**
    *   **Target Property:** **Ground State Electronic Energy.** This is a fundamental quantum property that influences binding. A lower energy often indicates a more stable molecule, which can be a useful proxy or filter.
    *   **Implementation (Simulated VQE):**
        1.  **Tool:** Use **Qiskit** (IBM's quantum SDK) with its built-in classical simulator, `qiskit_aer`.
        2.  **Process:** For a generated molecule (SMILES string):
            *   Use `RDKit` to generate a 3D geometry.
            *   Use the `PSI4` quantum chemistry package to compute the molecule's Hamiltonian (this is the most computationally expensive step, but it's standard for accuracy).
            *   **Simulate a VQE algorithm** using Qiskit to find the approximate ground state energy of that Hamiltonian.
        3.  **Key Point:** You are *simulating* what a quantum computer would do. This is perfectly valid for a proof-of-concept paper. You will state this clearly in the **Methods** section.

*   **Step 6: Integrate the Modules into QI-GAN.**
    *   **Action:** Modify the loss function of your baseline GAN (from Step 4).
    *   **How:** After the Generator produces a batch of molecules, feed them through your simulated VQE module. Then, add a **reward term** to the Generator's loss function that encourages lower predicted electronic energies.
    *   **Formula (Conceptual):** `Generator_Loss_QI = Adversarial_Loss - λ * (Energy_Reward)` where `λ` is a hyperparameter you tune.
    *   **Output:** Your final **QI-GAN model**.
